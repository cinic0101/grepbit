## Scope and ruler

Primary population is exactly the 95 distinct retained owner questions selected
by name-prevalence-01: Batch1 v15, Holdout2 run-26 and Holdout3 run-13 v14.
Reuse its identity joins, not its name-policy judgments. Hash files and match
case IDs/questions/verdict status. Do not select a better historical run.

Measure existing `absent_concepts` and `unmapped_concepts` separately. Distinguish
historically recorded refusal reason from current-predicate replay. Current
overlay and language pack do not retroactively describe old configuration.
No new definitions, negation rules, lexical exceptions or global bypass.

For full saved plans use their exact gate-relevant fields. Sanitized traces omit
ratio operands and latest/without details: use known references only as a lower
bound on mapping evidence. A fully mapped triggered concept is provably not
rejected by this positive-existence predicate, but an unmapped concept with
incomplete evidence stays unknown. Do not reconstruct arbitrary raw plans or
claim exact-plan replay from partial facts. No trigger is a separate observation,
not proof that the question was understood or that the plan was correct.

Historical answer/reference/verdict labels remain historical. Useful catches
and false refusals require independent intent/value evidence; otherwise report
affected historical labels and unresolved judgments, never invented new grades.
Without a saved proposal, a pre-planner refusal cannot prove what would have
been answered if that gate were absent.

Optional bounded supplement: the frozen 292-case product-baseline manifest,
excluding its six repeats, may be inventoried for **historical reason counts**
and existing refusal evidence. Keep authored/owner cohorts separate; the 50 owner
Batch1 cases overlap the primary population. Do not pool them as new questions.
Use this only to check blind spots in the owner-only, mainly POS/Chinese panel.
